# Tech Layoffs in 2026

## Article

[Tech Layoffs 2026: Tracking Job Losses Across Apple, Oracle, Uber, Meta, Microsoft and Others](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/breaking-news/article/tech-layoffs-2026-tracking-all-the-job-losses-across-apple-oracle-uber-tiktok-meta-microsoft-and-others-144545528.html?guccounter=1)

## Why I Found This Interesting

I found this article about tech layoffs and found it interesting because it shows how the technology industry is changing as companies invest more in AI. Many of the companies listed on here, such as Microsoft, Apple, Oracle, Meta, etc. have gone through layoffs in 2026. As a computer science student, this caught my attention because these are one of the main routes of employment for graduates and they employ probably the most software engineers. This worries me and makes me uncertain about my future, and makes me consider how to stand out in front of all these talented engineers.

Obviously, AI does not mean that software engineers will simply disappear, instead, the skills that are required of a software engineer might become different. SWE jobs might be rebranded as "AI Engineer", and as prompt engineering and other AI-usage related topics become increasingly useful, it makes me wonder what skills I should focus on and learn as developers increasingly work with AI tools, that gets stronger by the week.
